About EduMind & AI Concepts

Problem statement, scope, and viva-ready explanations

Problem Statement

Students in online or hybrid classrooms often disengage silently — looking away, using phones, or showing fatigue. Teachers cannot monitor every learner continuously. EduMind acts as an intelligent classroom observer that converts visual cues into logical facts and applies expert rules to infer behavior and recommend corrective actions.

Project Scope

  • Single-user laptop webcam monitoring
  • Behavior fact extraction (not raw CV decisions)
  • Prolog-based expert reasoning engine
  • Session analytics and recommendations
  • Educational GUI for demonstration and viva

Why OpenCV Does Not “Decide”

The CV layer answers: what is visible? (eyes closed, gaze away, hands near face). The Prolog layer answers: what does it mean? (sleepy, distracted, attentive). This separation is the hallmark of a classical expert system and distinguishes this project from a pure computer-vision application.
